abstract: |
  This article uses bibliometric methods to analyze the literature published in *Applied Psychology* since its inception in order to explore the characteristics of the journal. The results show that in recent years, the proportion of empirical research in *Applied Psychology* has declined, while the proportion of non-empirical research has increased. The proportion of funded research has increased year by year. There were 1,101 co-authored papers, and six clusters formed among highly productive authors. The main publishing institutions are primarily normal universities, with universities in Zhejiang publishing relatively more. The proportion of cross-institutional collaboration shows an overall upward trend. Citation and download frequencies are significantly positively correlated. Research hotspots have gone through three stages: an overview of the field of applied psychology, the psychology and behavior of different groups, and artificial intelligence. This study concludes that the journal has the following characteristics: (1) both "quality" and "quantity" advance together; (2) the trend of research collaboration is evident; (3) journal publications show regional patterns; and (4) social needs drive the development of *Applied Psychology*. In the future, the journal should continuously improve while maintaining its advantages, so as to better serve disciplinary development and meet social needs.
