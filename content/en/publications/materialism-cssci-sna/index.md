abstract: |
  Materialism—the emphasis on the importance of material wealth—originated in the cultural context of Western capitalism. Along with reform and opening up and the rapid development of the market economy, it has gradually become prevalent in contemporary Chinese society, attracting the attention and research interest of domestic scholars. To understand the landscape and characteristics of research in this field in China in recent years, this study used social network analysis to analyze keywords from materialism-related literature indexed in CSSCI. The results show that materialism is an interdisciplinary research topic; materialism research in China covers a broad range but is not concentrated. Materialism, materialistic values, college students, and well-being are core research topics in this field.
