The digital-intelligent transformation has become one of the main lifestyle characteristics of college students. Artificial intelligence represented by ChatGPT and DeepSeek has sprung up, marking that society has moved from the digital era to the digital-intelligent era. This requires college students not only to proactively embrace digital-intelligent technologies, but also to face the challenges that the digital-intelligent era brings to mental health.

In the digital-intelligent era, facing mental health challenges from multiple aspects at both the individual and group levels, college students' mental health education must innovate in both content and methods.
