abstract: |
  Open science can enable the effective sharing and full utilization of scientific resources and reshape research practices and culture, yet its development is still in its infancy. Taking psychological and brain sciences as examples, this article describes how this field, starting from enhancing the credibility and scientific rigor of research, spontaneously launched a grassroots open science movement, promoted open science practices throughout the entire research process in the field, and led interdisciplinary open science actions and cultural shaping. The article also analyzes the reasons for the vigorous development of open science practices in psychological and brain sciences, and provides recommendations for further promoting open science in China.
