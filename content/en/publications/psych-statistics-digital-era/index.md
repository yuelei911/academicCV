Faced with the characteristics and demands of the digital-intelligent era, change is the best remedy for responding to change. As one of the foundational methodology courses in psychology, psychological statistics needs to be the first to adapt quickly.

This article discusses the challenges and opportunities that digital-intelligent technologies bring to the teaching and research of psychological statistics, and explores how psychological statistics can proactively embrace the digital-intelligent future.
