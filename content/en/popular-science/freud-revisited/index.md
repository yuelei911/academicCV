This article invites readers to rediscover the great psychologist Freud, focusing on several lesser-known aspects of him. It introduces Freud's identities as a publishing company founder, a multiple-time Nobel Prize nominee, a collector of antiques and books, and a pioneer practitioner of animal-assisted therapy. Through these identities, the article presents a more three-dimensional and lifelike Freud, and demonstrates the depth and breadth spanned by his psychoanalytic thought.
